# Title: Combined sentiment + primary emotion classifier
# Version: 1 (Step 5). This is a SEPARATE prompt from sentiment_prompt.txt
# (which produced the validated Step 2 binary results). It does not modify it.

You are classifying the text of an Amazon product review. You are given the
review's title and body text. Produce TWO judgments for the reviewer's overall
expressed sentiment: a binary sentiment label and a single primary emotion.

## Allowed output (STRICT JSON)
Return exactly one JSON object with exactly these two keys, and nothing else:
  {"sentiment": "POSITIVE", "emotion": "joy"}
- "sentiment" must be exactly one of: POSITIVE, NEGATIVE
- "emotion" must be exactly one of the eight allowed labels:
  anger, anticipation, disgust, fear, joy, sadness, surprise, trust
Do not add prose, markdown fences, comments, or an extra key. The entire response
must be the JSON object only.

## Sentiment definitions
- POSITIVE: overall clearly favorable, satisfied, approving, or enthusiastic.
- NEGATIVE: overall dissatisfied, critical, or unfavorable; in this forced
  two-way choice, a review that LACKS a clearly favorable overall sentiment is
  NEGATIVE.

## Primary emotion definition
- "emotion" = the SINGLE emotion that best represents the reviewer's DOMINANT
  expressed emotional tone across the whole review (title + body).
- You may not use a ninth 'neutral'/'none' category. Choose one of the eight.

## Edge-case rules
- Empty or missing body text: judge from the title alone.
- Mixed emotions (e.g. praise plus frustration): pick the emotion that dominates
  the reviewer's tone overall; favor how the reviewer ends/emphasizes.
- Weak or emotionally flat text: still pick the emotion closest to the dominant
  tone; if nothing is clearly emotional, infer from the overall positive or
  negative thrust (satisfied -> trust or joy; dissatisfied -> sadness, anger,
  or disgust).
- Terse reviews: judge from whatever words are present.
- Several emotions plausible: pick the strongest, most salient one; tie-break
  toward the emotion that best matches the review's overall valence.

## Important
- You will NOT be given the product's star rating or any aggregate score, nor
  any other model's prediction. Judge only the title and body text provided.
- Output ONLY the JSON object.
