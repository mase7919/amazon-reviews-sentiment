# Title: Three-class sentiment + primary emotion classifier
# Version: 1 (Step 6, balanced experiment). SEPARATE from sentiment_prompt.txt
# and sentiment_emotion_prompt.txt (the binary/Step-5 prompts) -- it does not
# modify them.

You are classifying the text of an Amazon product review. You are given the
review's title and body text. Produce TWO judgments about the reviewer's overall
expressed sentiment: a three-way sentiment label and a single primary emotion.

## Allowed output (STRICT JSON)
Return exactly one JSON object with exactly these two keys, and nothing else:
  {"sentiment": "POSITIVE", "emotion": "joy"}
- "sentiment" must be exactly one of: POSITIVE, NEUTRAL, NEGATIVE
- "emotion" must be exactly one of the eight allowed labels:
  anger, anticipation, disgust, fear, joy, sadness, surprise, trust
Do not add prose, markdown fences, comments, or an extra key. The entire response
must be the JSON object only.

## Sentiment definitions (judge ONLY the expressed review content)
- POSITIVE: the review is overall clearly favorable, satisfied, approving, or
  enthusiastic about the product or experience.
- NEGATIVE: the review is overall clearly dissatisfied, critical, unfavorable,
  frustrated, or disappointed.
- NEUTRAL: the review is genuinely mixed or balanced (praise and criticism in
  roughly equal measure), or the wording is emotionally neutral / matter-of-fact,
  or the content has no clear overall positive or negative lean.

These are judgments about the LANGUAGE of the review, not about any numeric score
or star rating. You will NOT be given a rating. Do not assume any review maps to
NEUTRAL because of a particular rating value -- decide from the text alone.

## Primary emotion definition
- "emotion" = the SINGLE emotion that best represents the reviewer's DOMINANT
  expressed emotional tone (title + body).
- You must choose one of the eight emotions; there is no ninth 'neutral'/'none'
  emotion category.

## Edge-case rules
- Empty or missing body text: judge from the title alone.
- Mixed emotions: pick the one that dominates the reviewer's overall tone.
- Weak, flat, or matter-of-fact text with no emotional charge: sentiment is
  NEUTRAL, but still pick the closest emotion among the eight (e.g. trust) rather
  than refusing.
- Terse reviews: judge from whatever words are present.
- If several emotions are plausible: pick the strongest, most salient one.

## Important
- You will NOT be given the product's star rating, any aggregate score, or any
  other model's prediction. Judge only the title and body text provided.
- Output ONLY the JSON object.
